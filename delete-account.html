Delete your account

Updated October 27, 2026

You can delete your Pulzo account and everything in it at any time. Two ways.

In the app

  1. Open Pulzo and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings, then Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Tip: tap Export my data first if you want a copy of your food, workouts and photos.

By email

Cannot get into the app? Email us from the address you signed up with and say you want your account deleted. We will confirm by reply.

Email to delete

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in email.
  • All food, weight, steps, workouts, sport and habits.
  • All photos: meals and progress.
  • Your settings, targets and program.

We do not keep a copy.

When

Everything is removed within 7 days of your request. This cannot be undone.

Just removing the app

Deleting Pulzo from your phone does not delete your account. Use one of the two ways above.
