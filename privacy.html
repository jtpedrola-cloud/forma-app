Pulzo

Privacy

Updated October 27, 2026

Pulzo is a personal tracker. What you log is yours. This page says what we keep, where, and who can see it.

What we store

  • Your email, so you can sign in with a link. There is no password.
  • Your food: what you ate, with calories and macros.
  • Your weight, steps, workouts, sport and habits.
  • Photos you add: meals and progress photos.
  • Your settings, targets and program.

Where it is kept

In our database and photo storage at Supabase, in the Singapore region. Your data is sent over an encrypted connection and each account can only open its own records. Photos are in private storage, not public links.

Who sees it

Only you. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers or show ads. The person who runs Pulzo can reach the database to keep it working, and does not browse your entries except to fix a problem you report.

AI meal estimates

If you ask Pulzo for an AI estimate of a meal, the photo or text for that one request is sent to Claude, an AI service by Anthropic, so it can name the food and portion. Nothing is sent unless you ask. There is a small daily limit on AI estimates.

Export and delete

You can download everything you logged as a zip: open Settings, then Account, then Export my data.

You can delete your account and all its data at any time. See how to delete your account. Everything is removed within 7 days.

Children

Pulzo is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If we change this page in a way that matters, we will say so in the app and update the date at the top.
